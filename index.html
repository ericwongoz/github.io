Personal Photo Transfer

A private tool for copying one person's own photo library into their own Google Drive.

What it does

Personal Photo Transfer runs on its owner's Mac. Each night it copies the owner's photos and videos from their Amazon Photos account into their own Google Drive, at full original quality, until the whole library has been copied.

To avoid copying anything twice, it reads the names, sizes and checksums of the files already in the owner's Google Drive, and skips photos that are already there. It only adds files: it never changes or deletes anything in Google Drive.

Who it is for

This is a personal project, used only by its owner with their own Google account. It is not offered as a service, and no one else's data is accessed.

Privacy

Nothing is sent anywhere except to Amazon and Google, and no data is shared with anyone. Read the privacy policy.
