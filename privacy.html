Privacy Policy

Personal Photo Transfer · Effective 3 November 2026

Personal Photo Transfer is a private tool that its owner runs on their own Mac. It copies the owner's photos and videos from Amazon Photos into the owner's own Google Drive. It is used only by its owner, with their own Google account, and it is not offered to anyone else.

Google user data the app accesses

With the owner's permission (the Google Drive scope), the app:

  • lists the files in the owner's Google Drive, with their names, folders, sizes and checksums, to find photos that are already there and avoid copying them twice;
  • uploads photo and video files into a folder in the owner's Google Drive;
  • reads the account's storage usage, to check there is room before uploading.

The app never changes, overwrites or deletes existing files in Google Drive.

How the data is used and stored

  • All processing happens on the owner's Mac. The app has no server, and it sends nothing anywhere except to Google and Amazon.
  • File names, folder paths, sizes and checksums are kept in a local database on that Mac, so the copy can resume and be checked.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored on the Mac in an encrypted file. Its password is kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind is used.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone. It is used only to provide the copying described above.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's data folder on the Mac removes everything it stored there.
